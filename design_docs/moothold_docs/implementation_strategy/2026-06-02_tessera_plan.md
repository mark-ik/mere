## The one idea

Tessera is a **receipt of followed-through commitment**, not a coin. You cannot buy it; you earn it by doing what you said (hosting, pinning, governance) and lose it by visibly failing to (ghosting a commitment). It accrues against your **persona-chain root**, not the leaf persona, so reputation is the cost of continuity and anonymity is the cost of starting over. Like everything in the substrate, tessera is **event-sourced**: signed tessera events on the DAG are authoritative; the score is a projection.

The Roman sense (tessera hospitalis, a guest-friendship token) is the right metaphor: a durable, transferable-by-vouching token of standing.

## What tessera is not

- **Not cash.** You cannot deposit money to make the network preserve your stuff or admit you (moot tiers §3). Compute-credit uses (geist models brief) are a *use* of earned tessera, never a purchase of it. The plan must keep tessera unbuyable.
- **Not the policy engine.** Tessera is the *facts* (score, freshness, role). The policy decision ("may this contributor pin this cluster under current moot rules?") is the Biscuit candidate's job (§8.8). Keep them separate so the reputation model and the authorization language evolve independently.
- **Not a single global number, probably.** See the open question on global-vs-per-moot below.

## Tessera event grammar (the authoritative layer)

Tessera-affecting events are signed MereEvents/operations on the event-DAG (same wire as murm posts, same `cabal_id`/space addressing). First-cut event types:

- **CommitmentMade** — a pledge (host a service, pin a cluster, take a role) with scope (cluster-path), heartbeat cadence, and duration.
- **Heartbeat** — renews a live commitment ("still hosting"); absence past cadence = lapse.
- **CommitmentFulfilled** / **CommitmentLapsed** — the honey and the stick. Fulfilled raises tessera; lapse (silent disappearance) lowers it. Lapse is detected from missing heartbeats, not self-reported.
- **CleanHandoff** — transfer a commitment to a willing successor with no rep penalty (walk away cleanly).
- **Vouch** — delegate a fraction of the voucher's tessera to a newcomer as an entry boost; signed; abuse loops back to the voucher's own score.
- **GovernanceParticipation** — voting and other participation the moot rewards.

The **reciprocity ledger** (t3 ILL credits between moots) is a sibling on the same machinery; open question whether it is the same ledger or a parallel one keyed by moot rather than persona.

## The score (the projection)

A materialized tessera score per **chain root**, computed from the event stream:

- **Accrual against the chain root, not the leaf.** Persona keys derive from `master + persona_id` (`derive_keypair(blake3("persona" || persona_id))`); the chain links a persona to its parent; the public identity is the leaf. Tessera is keyed by the chain root so a user's standing follows them across persona forks.
- **Persona-fork depreciation.** A new persona on an existing chain inherits a *fraction* of the root's tessera (a depreciation curve). A brand-new chain starts at zero. This is the Sybil floor: fresh chains have nothing.
- **Decay / recency.** Tessera should likely decay or recency-weight so standing reflects current behavior, not a one-time burst (open question on the curve).

## Phases (done-conditions, not dates)

### Phase 1 — Event grammar + ledger projection
- Define the tessera event types above as MereEvent extensions; implement the projection that folds them into a per-chain-root score.
- Pure data model; testable without network (fold a sequence of events → expected scores, including lapse detection from missing heartbeats).
- **Done when:** a deterministic event sequence yields the expected tessera scores, and lapse/fulfilment/handoff move the score as designed. **✅ Done 2026-06-02** — [`moothold::tessera`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera): `TesseraEvent` grammar (`event.rs`) + the `Ledger` projection (`ledger.rs`); lapse derived from missing heartbeats vs a supplied clock; deterministic integer math (basis-point fractions, no floats) so peers agree; 10 tests (fulfilment up, lapse down, lapse-and-revive, clean-handoff moves the liability not a penalty, fulfilment-after-handoff rewards the successor, owner-only liveness, vouch boost, governance, mixed sequence). The operation-wire bridge (tessera events as signed operation extensions on the synced DAG) is deferred to a follow-up, exactly as murmuring split `Post` from its `Operation` wire.

### Phase 2 — Persona chain + accrual/depreciation
- Wire the score to the persona chain root (identity vault + persona model). Implement fork inheritance (fraction `f`) and new-chain-zero.
- **Done when:** a forked persona inherits `f ·` root tessera; a fresh chain starts at zero; the chain root is the stable key across forks. **✅ Done 2026-06-02** — [`tessera::persona_chain`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/persona_chain.rs): `PersonaChains` (a leaf→parent forest) resolves a persona to `(ChainRoot, depth)` and presents an `effective_score` = the root's ledger score depreciated by `depreciation_bp` once per generation (`f^depth`, default 0.5). The accrual `Ledger` stays root-keyed + unchanged (clean layering: accrual core vs presentation layer). 8 tests (root full, fork = `f·root`, deeper forks `f^depth`, fresh chain = 0, cycle-safe). **Design call beyond the plan:** only *positive* standing depreciates — debt/zero carries fully to forks, so forking can't launder a bad reputation (the only escape is a fresh chain = 0, below threshold). This realizes "anonymity is the cost of starting over"; flagged for review. `f^depth` (a curve) was chosen over flat-per-fork; time-decay remains an open question. Identity-vault wiring (`PersonaId` ↔ `master + persona_id` chain) is a thin host adapter, deferred like Phase 1's operation-wire bridge.

### Phase 3 — Vouching as delegation
- Signed `Vouch` events transfer a fraction of the voucher's tessera as a newcomer boost; abuse loops back to the voucher (their score carries the newcomer's bad behavior).
- **Done when:** a vouch raises a newcomer above a moot's posting threshold, and a vouched newcomer's misbehavior measurably costs the voucher. **✅ Done 2026-06-02** — the boost landed in Phase 1; Phase 3 added the loop-back: the ledger records vouch edges (`vouchers_of`), and a `Censure` of a vouchee loops `vouch_loopback_bp` of the censure back onto each direct voucher (one hop). A test proves a vouched newcomer's censure measurably costs the voucher.

### Phase 4 — Gates (the §8.8 policy slot)
- Expose tessera facts (score, role, heartbeat freshness, quota usage) to the policy layer. Implement moot posting thresholds, per-moot rate limits, and velocity-based detection as policy checks over those facts.
- This is where tessera meets the **structural caps** (already proven, Probe 2 / spike Follow-up A): a member may act iff a structural cap covers the cluster-path **and** the policy facts (tessera/quota/heartbeat/role) permit it. Structural cap = *where*; policy facts = *whether right now*.
- **Done when:** an action is allowed only when both a valid cap covers it and the tessera facts pass; a fresh-chain flood is thrown out by threshold + rate limit. **✅ Done 2026-06-02** — [`tessera::gate`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/gate.rs): `TesseraFacts` (the score + recent-action timestamps a moot assembles) and `may_act(cap_covers, facts, now, GateConfig) -> GateDecision`, which refuses `NoCapability` first, then `BelowThreshold` (a fresh chain at 0), then `RateLimited` (a burst past the window limit). The reference policy; the Biscuit *language* wrapping it is the sibling plan.

### Phase 5 — Federation: moot concords + composite reputation (t3)

**Resolved (2026-06-02, with Mark):** reputation is **per-moot primary; "global" reputation is a concord-weighted composition of per-moot standings.** There is no single authoritative global score. A persona has a standing *in each moot* (the Phase 1 ledger + Phase 2 depreciation, scoped to that moot's events), and any viewer moot composes a cross-moot view from the standings of the moots it has agreed to honor.

**Concord.** A concord is a signed agreement in which a viewer moot honors another moot's reputations at a weight (basis points), **one-directional** and **one-hop**: a moot honors its *direct* concords, never its concords' concords. One-hop is the safety rule — transitive trust would launder reputation down a chain of agreements (the classic web-of-trust failure). The concord graph carries the federation topology: moots in the same **moothold** are allied and get strong / near-automatic concords (reputation is fungible inside the moothold); a **coalition** (t4 coalition) gets weaker, negotiated cross-moothold concords; unrelated moots are zero until they sign something.

**Composite reputation** as viewer moot V sees persona P: V's own effective score for P, plus a contribution from each moot V concords, combined under V's chosen **composition policy**. The policy is a **decision for the people running the moot, not a project default** (Mark, 2026-06-02): a moot picks how trusting or cautious it is. Initial policy set (extensible):
- **Insular** — local only; concords ignored. ("I judge behavior in my house.")
- **WeightedSum** — local + Σ weight · concorded standing. ("I trust my allies' read in proportion to the concord.")
- **CautiousImport** — like WeightedSum, but a concorded moot's *negative* read counts at full weight while positive reads are discounted by the concord. ("A red flag is a red flag; praise from elsewhere I discount.") The anti-laundering posture.

A moot wanting local-primary just keeps its concord weights low (local has weight 1); a moot can ignore the world with `Insular` or with no concords.

**Anti-laundering, now self-policing.** A pardon or inflated standing in moot X reaches P elsewhere only through the moots that concord X, weighted, one hop. A rogue rep-farm with no concords launders nothing; a moot that concords a junk moot pollutes its *own* view of everyone, so it is incentivised to concord carefully. The guard falls out of the same mechanism that enables sharing, and is self-correcting. (This supersedes Phase 2's blunt "debt never depreciates" as the *cross-moot* guard; within a single moot, Phase 2's rule still holds.)

**Absolution falls out.** "Can a community absolve your bad reputation?" — yes: a per-moot governance-authorised `Pardon` event (the mirror of `Vouch`) improving P's standing *in that moot*; how far it carries is exactly how far that moot's concords reach. Self-issued clearing stays blocked (laundering); community-issued clearing is redemption, scoped by the trust graph. Its symmetric twin is `Censure` (a moot marking P down for in-house behaviour beyond ghosting a pledge); both are the same governance-signed per-moot machinery, a follow-on event-grammar addition.

**The concord generalises past reputation.** A rep concord is one facet of a broader inter-moot agreement: moots may also concord on **schema** (shared / normalised tessera config — a precondition, since standings computed under different weights are not comparable), **reciprocity** (the ILL-shaped pin / host credits, the original Phase 5 sense), **data, compute, model weights, links, feeds.** The facets have a dependency order: rep-sharing sits on schema-sharing; feeds and links sit on rep. This plan builds the reputation facet; the others are siblings on the same concord primitive.

- **Done when:** the composite-reputation core is real — per-moot ledgers + the concord graph + a viewer's composition policy fold to a deterministic composite score, one-hop, with the policy a moot-operator choice. Slices, all ✅ landed 2026-06-02 (see Progress): the `Pardon` / `Censure` events (community absolution + judgement); **schema-concord gating** (`composite_score` drops a concord whose moot scores under a different `TesseraConfig` — standings on different scales are not comparable); the **reciprocity-credit facet** ([`tessera::reciprocity`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/reciprocity.rs): directed give-and-take credits between moots, `may_request` cuts off a freeloader whose unreciprocated debt exceeds tolerance — privileges, not money).

## Findings

- **Tessera is already specified in shape, just unbuilt.** The substrate brief §8.7/§8.9 and the moot tiers brief §4/§7/§10 converge on: event-sourced, chain-root-keyed, fork-depreciating, vouch-delegable, gating posting/hosting/reciprocity, honey-and-stick with visible-failure-not-coercion. This plan consolidates that into an event grammar + projection + phase ladder.
- **Tessera is the facts source, Biscuit is the policy brain.** The §8.8 stack is explicit: structural caps (meadowcap-shaped, ours, proven) + policy authorization (Biscuit candidate, consumes tessera facts) + group/key state (p2panda-encryption, proven). Tessera fills the facts half of the middle slot; the Biscuit wiring is a sibling plan.
- **Sequence after LogSync.** Tessera events are operations on the synced event-DAG; the score projection is exactly the kind of materialized view the sync-as-projection plan stands up. Build the sync substrate first, then tessera rides it.
- **Reputation is per-moot; "global" is a concord composition (2026-06-02, with Mark).** The cleanest resolution of global-vs-per-moot is to refuse the choice: per-moot standings are primary (a moot's ledger + depreciation), and cross-moot reputation is a *composition* a viewer moot assembles from the moots it concords — one-directional, one-hop, under its own composition policy. Three consequences make it the right model. (1) Anti-laundering becomes **self-policing**: a junk moot's inflation reaches only its direct concords, and a moot that concords junk pollutes its own view, so no global lock is needed. (2) Reputation fungibility **maps onto the federation topology** for free — within a moothold it is high (allies), across a coalition it is negotiated, between strangers it is zero. (3) "Community absolution" becomes a **per-moot `Pardon`** whose reach is exactly the concord graph (redemption, not self-service laundering). The composition rule is explicitly a **moot-operator choice, not a project default** — Mere ships a policy set (Insular / WeightedSum / CautiousImport), moots pick. The concord generalises: reputation is one facet of an inter-moot agreement that also covers schema (a precondition for comparable scores), reciprocity, data, compute, feeds.
- **Tessera networks today, proven by a running two-peer convergence (2026-06-03).** A standalone probe ([`crates/probes/tessera-logsync`](../../../crates/probes/tessera-logsync)) replicates a moot's tessera event log between two real p2panda-net peers and shows both fold the synced operations to the identical score. The wire bridge ([`tessera::wire`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/wire.rs)) plus the [`Ledger`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/ledger.rs) projection are network-correct, not just unit-correct: tessera rides the same LogSync substrate as murm posts. The store is now productized into moothold ([`TesseraStore`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/store.rs) + [`SyncedMoot`](../../../crates/moot/moothold/src/tessera/sync.rs), 2026-06-03); what is left is host wiring, not tessera design.

## Open questions

- **Global vs per-moot tessera. RESOLVED (2026-06-02, with Mark): per-moot primary; "global" is a concord-weighted composition** — see Phase 5. A persona's standing is earned per-moot; any viewer moot composes a cross-moot view from the moots it concords (one-hop, weighted) under a moot-operator-chosen composition policy. Anti-laundering becomes self-policing via the trust graph rather than a global lock.
- **Depreciation curve + decay shape.** What fraction `f` does a forked persona inherit, and does tessera decay over time? Per-moot configurable with defaults (moot tiers §8), but the defaults need design.
- **Vouch fraction + duration.** How much tessera a vouch transfers, and for how long the boost lasts (moot tiers §12).
- **Fork tessera carryover on moot fork.** When a *moot* forks, do member tesseras carry to the fork, both, at what ratio (moot tiers §12).
- **Reciprocity ledger: same or sibling.** Folded into the concord model (Phase 5): reciprocity (ILL pin / host credits) is *one facet* of an inter-moot concord, a sibling to the reputation facet on the same agreement primitive. Open sub-question: whether reciprocity credits are their own keyed-by-moot ledger or ride the same event machinery.
- **Compute-credit boundary.** Tessera-as-compute-credit (geist models) must not become a back-door to *buy* tessera. Define the one-way valve (earn tessera → spend on compute; never buy tessera with compute/cash).

## Pitfalls

- **Tessera gates almost everything, so it is a single point of design risk.** Mitigate by keeping it a pure facts source behind the policy engine, and by making thresholds per-moot configurable (no global hardcoded reputation economy).
- **Buyability creep.** Any feature that lets tessera be acquired without earned follow-through breaks the whole anti-plutocracy premise. Guard it explicitly.
- **Privacy/continuity trade-off is intended, not a bug.** Delete-the-chain-and-start-fresh costing all reputation is the design; do not "fix" it by making tessera portable across unlinked chains.
