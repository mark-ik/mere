# tulpa

The legend and memorial layer: what memory makes of history.

Legends, memorials, epithets, and the manifestations of the dead. A tulpa's
substance is attention — its presence scales with how often it is retold, and
a legend nobody tells fades.

The boundaries are the point:

- **Not ordinary event history.** That is [`codicil`](https://github.com/merely-made/mere),
  the append-only log of what actually happened. A tulpa is a *view* over such
  a log, holding the retold subset.
- **Not descent.** That is [`fili`](https://crates.io/crates/fili), which
  records continuity of line across worlds. Where `fili` tracks who carried the
  line, `tulpa` holds what is remembered when nobody did.

Named from Tibetan *sprul-pa* by way of two westernizations, and chosen to
complete a triad the stack already held: **servitor** (created and bounded) →
**tulpa** (remembered and autonomous) → **egregore** (collective and
emergent).
